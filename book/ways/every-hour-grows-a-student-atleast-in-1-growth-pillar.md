
# Every hour grows a student in at least one growth pillar

Take a printed timetable for any week and go hour by hour with one question: which growth pillar does this hour grow? The seven pillars are the dimensions we develop in a student: Foundational Learning, Computer Science Readiness, Professional Readiness, Productivity & Self-Management, Work Integration, Community & Leadership, and Values & Ethics. An hour might grow two or three at once. A project review touches craft, communication and self-management together.

The test is finding the hour that grows none. The assembly held because it has always been held. The session scheduled because the room and the slot were both free. The waiting, the repeated announcements, the filler.

Those hours are not neutral. We mandate a student's presence 8 hours a day, 9 to 6, Monday to Saturday. A mandated hour that grows nothing teaches something anyway: that our schedule is padded, and that being present and being developed are different things here. Students notice the pattern, and it costs us the seriousness of the hours that do count.

An hour that fails the test has two honest futures. Redesign it until it grows something, or give it back to the student, whose own use of a free hour beats our use of a wasted one.

Run the test on this week's timetable. How many hours pass?
