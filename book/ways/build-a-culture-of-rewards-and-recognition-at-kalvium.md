
# Build a culture of rewards and recognition at Kalvium

"I did my job perfectly. Why didn't anyone clap?"

You will hear this, usually from someone new. School trained them for it: show up, get an attendance award, finish the project, get a certificate. So decide early what your campus claps for. People watch what gets applauded, work out what this place values, and repeat it.

When a pilot lands the plane safely, the passengers do not stand and cheer. That was the job. Submitting working code, attending sessions, meeting the sprint, these are the baseline at Kalvium, and we do not celebrate the baseline. Applaud it and you teach everyone that average is excellent.

What we recognize, loudly and constantly, is culture. The student who stayed back to help a struggling peer. The one who found a bug in the curriculum and flagged it so others would not lose an evening. The one who was drifting last month, owned it without excuses, and turned around. Outcomes can get an ad-hoc cheer, a genuine offer or a shipped project deserves one, but the institution's memory and its loudest praise go to how people acted on the way.

Keep recognition and reward distinct. Recognition affirms who someone is becoming, and anyone can give it, a note, a shout-out in standup, a peer saying what they saw. Reward is access, harder problems, wider responsibility, a seat closer to the decisions, and it follows demonstrated culture rather than being dangled ahead of it. When you recognize, be specific and praise the action. "The way you explained recursion to Priya was clear and patient" teaches. "You were great today" does not.

One caution. The moment a student asks "if I help my peer, do I get extra credit?", the system has tipped into transaction. Recognition works when it lands on behaviour someone chose without knowing anyone was watching. Turn it into a rate card and the behaviour follows the card, not the value.

For students this is a slow conversion, they arrive having only ever been recognized for outcomes. What did your campus clap for this week?
