
# Function + Soul? (heart)

Some week soon, a part of your work here will turn boring. Grading the same submission pattern for the fortieth time. Another induction session. The same escalation, again.

The usual explanation is that the task is boring, and the usual fix is to wait for a better task. Look at the extremes and both fall apart. A person can polish shoes, a task most people file under lowly, decide to do it perfectly, and find real joy in the result. Another person can spend the day working with AI, tools that were science fiction a few years ago, and feel nothing. It is not the external thing. It is within your mind.

So when a task turns boring, the practical move is to raise your attention on it, not to lower your effort while waiting for something better. Try to do it as well as it can be done. At full attention the fortieth submission stops being a repeat, because you start asking what this student misunderstood that the other thirty-nine did not, and that question was sitting in the task the whole time. Going through the motions is function. The same motions with your heart in them is function with soul, and the person receiving the work can usually tell which one they got.

You are going to spend the hour on the task either way. What is the most boring part of your week, and what would doing it perfectly look like?
