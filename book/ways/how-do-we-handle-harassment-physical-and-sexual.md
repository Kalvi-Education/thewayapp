
# How do we handle harassment, physical and sexual

Zero tolerance. No exceptions for seniority, for talent, or for how much the person otherwise contributes.

If someone reports harassment to you, your role is narrow and urgent. Listen. Do not investigate, do not mediate between the parties, and do not decide for yourself how credible it sounds. Escalate the same day, through the formal channel your campus has been given.

Those limits are not caution for its own sake. Handling a complaint informally, however well meant, can destroy the evidence, expose the person who reported, and put the organisation outside what the law requires of it. This is one of the few areas where acting on your own judgment makes things worse.

What we owe in return is a process that is real: a named channel, trained people running it, and protection for the person who reports, set out in a policy document rather than left to the goodwill of whoever is on duty. If you cannot name that channel for your campus today, treat that as urgent and raise it now, before it is needed.
