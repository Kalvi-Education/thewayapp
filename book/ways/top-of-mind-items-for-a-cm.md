
# Top of mind items for a CM

A Campus Manager's day fills itself. Emails, escalations, a university meeting, a broken AC. If you let the inbox decide what you think about, a month passes and you cannot say whether your campus got better. So carry a fixed list of obsessions, and return to it every day. Ours has three groups.

**Your mentors.** How are they conducting sessions? How are they evaluating? Are they showing up every day, prepared? A campus is only ever as good as its mentors that week.

**Your students' growth, on every axis.** How are they faring in subjects, in coding, in communication, in careers, in effort, in overall growth? Not one number but several lenses, because a student can look fine on marks while their effort quietly collapses.

**Your campus as a place.** How disciplined is it, starting with everyday attendance for students and mentors alike? Is there a culture of help, where asking is normal? Are wins being rewarded and recognised, for students and mentors? Are you spotting and growing the leaders of tomorrow? And is there fun, events, actual life here? A student does not put four years of effort into a place they cannot wait to leave each day.

The list is deliberately about leading indicators. By the time a lagging metric drops, the causes have been building in exactly these places, out of view.

Run the list against today. Which item did you not touch at all?
