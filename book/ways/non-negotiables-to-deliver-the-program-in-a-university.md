
# Non-negotiables to deliver the program in a university

We co-run the program with universities, and the partnership works because the roles are clear.

The university provides the ground: infrastructure, administration, compliance, and a campus life our students belong to fully. Sports, clubs, events, facilities, all of it. Our students learn differently, they do not belong differently.

Kalvium owns the academic engine: curriculum, pedagogy and student evaluation. These are not open to partner adjustment. A university asking us to soften an evaluation or reshape the pedagogy is asking us to break the promise we made to the student, and the answer is no, said respectfully and early.

We are not a vendor delivering a service, and the university is not a landlord. We are collaborators with clearly divided roles, and holding the division clearly is what lets the partnership run for years without corroding. Much of the friction between a campus team and a university traces back to one side drifting across this line.

If something feels tense with your university partner right now, which side of the line is being crossed?
