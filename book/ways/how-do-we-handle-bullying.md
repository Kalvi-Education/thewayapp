
# How do we handle bullying?

Zero tolerance, on every campus.

If you see bullying, or a student reports it to you, escalate it. Do not wait until you are sure it meets some definition of real bullying. Checking a report that turns out to be nothing costs you an hour. Missing one that was real costs a student far more, and teaches the rest of the room that reporting goes nowhere.

The other failure is quieter. A campus can decide to handle it internally to protect its own image, and the student learns the same lesson.

Consequences for bullying and care for the student who reported both have to be visible on your campus. If either is missing where you work, that is worth raising before the next incident, not after.
