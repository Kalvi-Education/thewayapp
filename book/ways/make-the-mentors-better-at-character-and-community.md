
# Make the mentors better at character and community

We can teach Python with lectures and labs. Now try teaching integrity with a slide deck.

Character does not transfer through content. It is caught, from watching how the adults in the system behave when behaving well is inconvenient. Students watch how a mentor treats a struggling colleague, whether the person enforcing deadlines meets their own, what happens when a mistake surfaces. To a student in the lab, the central team is an abstraction and the documents are theory. The campus team is what they actually see, so the campus team is what they learn from.

Two habits to build in your mentors, and in yourself.

First, build community, not service. A service provider treats students like customers: keep them happy, shield them from difficulty, protect the rating. Watch what that does under pressure. Imagine a cohort stuck on a hard integration, anxiety high. The service instinct produces a simplified cheat sheet that bypasses the difficult logic. Students are relieved, the mentor feels helpful, and the lesson learned is: when things get hard, find a backdoor. The community builder instead sits down and says, this is genuinely tough, I do not know the answer yet either, let us work through the documentation together. That answer normalises struggle and teaches that getting stuck is engineering, not failure.

Second, hold consistency between the stage and the cafeteria. A mentor who enforces punctuality in class and then rolls their eyes about "the central team's attendance policy" over lunch has cancelled their own teaching. On standards and conduct, the lunch table teaches as much as the lab, so what you model there counts too. Disagreements and concerns still have their place, in the forums built for them, argued properly. Small language shifts help here: "we don't do that here" builds identity where "you are not allowed" merely polices.

This is also an institutional duty, and it runs through the CM, the Campus Manager. A mentor arriving late with no comment from anyone resets the standard for the whole campus. Correcting it kindly and immediately is part of the CM's job, every time.
